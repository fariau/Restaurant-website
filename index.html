FAQ

Good to know

Are the prices inclusive of tax?

No. Buffet prices are per person and tax is added on top.

Which children get the kids’ price?

The kids’ price applies to children aged 4 to 7.

Is there vegetarian food?

Yes, the buffet includes vegetarian dishes such as chana and aloo tarkari, plus salads and desserts. The line-up rotates, so call ahead if you are after something specific.

Can I order takeaway?

Chaupal is a dine-in buffet. For takeaway or catering, please call the branch.

How do I pay?

Payment is settled at the restaurant after your meal.
